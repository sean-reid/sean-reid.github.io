Meta Tic-Tac-Toe

Ultimate tic-tac-toe takes a game that's been solved since childhood and makes it hard. The board is a 3x3 grid of smaller boards, and the cell you play in decides which board your opponent must play in next.

The opponent is an alpha-beta search written in Rust and compiled to WebAssembly.
