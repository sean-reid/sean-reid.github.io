ooooh

I think better out loud, but the usual note-taking apps are just speaking into the void, with no feedback. So it's difficult to form a coherent note sometimes. Chatbots fill that gap partially, but speak too frequently. That's why I built ooooh.

Asking at the right time

Early versions would interrupt me. I tried having the voice pause when I started to speak, but on speakerphone it would catch some of its own audio as me talking and stop itself.

The balance was to have it speak less often, be really sure I've finished talking, and then confidently say something without listening to itself.

A lot of the time went into calibrating Jev so the questions come at the right moment. I recorded about ten sessions and used my experience with them to guide the tuning.

Using it

I use it to take voice notes. A small but mighty group of beta testers gives me feedback before I launch, mostly on the quality of the questions.
