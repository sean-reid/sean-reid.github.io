Gardener's Dilemma

  • Five vines, each a number. Every 2.5 seconds every vine takes one Collatz step: halve it if even, triple it and add 1 if odd.
  • Harvesting a vine scores its current value, and harvests that follow the same Collatz path chain for a multiplier.
  • The dilemma is whether to grab the tall vine now or wait for one that chains. A vine at 76 will be 38 on the next tick.
