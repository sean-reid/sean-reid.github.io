Points

In 5th grade I played a game called Green Globs. Green dots were scattered on a coordinate plane and I had to type an equation whose graph passed through as many as possible. Points does the reverse, where I click a few points on a grid and the computer finds a curve through all of them, as an exact implicit equation with small integer coefficients like x² + y² = 25 or x · y = 6.
