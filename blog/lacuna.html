Lacuna

Lacuna starts as an empty circle with 12 anchor points around the rim. Two players take turns drawing strands inside it, and the strands become the board. When a region ends up bounded entirely by one player's strands, it seals and scores for that player.

I designed the game and its rules from scratch. You could play it with two pens and a printed circle, but tracking which regions have closed is tedious by hand, and the web version does that for you. Source.
