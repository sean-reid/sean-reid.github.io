Random Game

  • There's an icebreaker where someone picks a number in a range, say 1 to 100, everyone guesses, and the closest wins.
  • Alone it's binary search. With other people guessing it's a land grab.
  • I built the first version because I was guessing "intuitively" and competing with everyone else's intuition for the same part of the number line.
  • Most people guess round numbers.
