OEIS Pi Search

  • Type an OEIS A-number, a sequence name, a few terms, or a string of digits, and the site tells you how far into pi you have to go before the first \(k\) terms appear.
  • The search covers the first 1,000,000,000 digits and strings up to 12 digits long.
