Wikipedia Unscrambler

The typoglycemia meme says you can read jumbled text as long as the first and last letters stay put. That's an overstatement, but context does a lot of the work when you read, and I wanted a game that measured how much. So this scrambles every letter of every word and gives you nothing but the words around it.

The passages come from yesterday's most-read Wikipedia articles, so the puzzles rotate on their own.
